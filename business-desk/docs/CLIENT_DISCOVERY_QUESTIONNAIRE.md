# Client discovery and configuration worksheet

Use this with each pilot owner **and** the staff member doing the daily work. Ask them to demonstrate recent examples. Record observations separately from requested features. Do not request real customer identity documents, passwords or unrestricted database copies during an interview.

Owner decisions already confirmed: free pilot with paid plans later; all four profiles; offline drafts with server-confirmed money entries.

## Questions for the product owner

1. Which real pilot businesses can participate? For each: profile, country/city, contact role and intended starting workflow.
2. What is the app's customer-facing name? Should each business see its own name, your brand, or both?
3. Who provides onboarding and support, in which languages, and during what hours?
4. Who pays for hosting, offsite backup and optional messaging during the free pilot? What monthly operating cap is acceptable?
5. Which domain/provider will host the first private instances? Who is the operator responsible for recovery?
6. What event should trigger discussing a paid plan: a fixed pilot period, successful acceptance, or a measured business outcome?
7. Which commitment may you actually make to customers: business-hour support, next-day response, or round-the-clock coverage?
8. Can you provide the latest working quotation app's source/export and a checklist of its behaviours? The older repository alone cannot verify feature parity.

## Business identity and context

9. What does this business sell or do? Which three tasks produced the most revenue last month?
10. Is it a garage/collision shop, showroom, retailer/trader, service team, or a combination? Which work deserves its opening screen?
11. What are the legal name, trading name, country and operating locations? Is one organisation responsible for all locations?
12. Who handles tax/accounting? What registration applies, and which bill/credit formats have they approved?
13. Which staff members create, approve, bill, collect, receive stock and review payroll? Who may see costs or salaries?
14. How many simultaneous staff, counters and devices are used at peak? Are phones shared?
15. Which languages can the actual users read comfortably? Which language do clients prefer for contact?
16. Which fields do staff genuinely use? Which existing forms are unnecessarily long?

## Walk through a real day's work

17. Show the last customer from enquiry through completion and collection. Where was information typed twice?
18. What does the owner ask staff every morning? Which answers are currently unreliable?
19. What was the latest forgotten promise? Who was responsible, and where was its date recorded?
20. Show the last incorrect invoice. Was the problem price, quantity, tax, approval, customer identity or payment status?
21. What happens at the busiest hour? How long should a normal repeat-customer bill take?
22. How does a new staff member learn the work? What requires asking the owner?
23. What must stay usable without internet? Is the local Wi-Fi/network itself dependable?
24. Which computers, phone browsers, scanners and printer models must be supported? Bring a sample A4/thermal output.

## Customers, credit and communication

25. How do you recognise the same customer—phone, registration, account code, company name or another identifier?
26. Can one customer have several vehicles/assets/sites and several contact people?
27. Which facts personalise the service: preferred part/oil, warranty, site access, fleet contract, invoice address or buying pattern?
28. Who agrees discounts and credit terms? Are they percentages, fixed item prices, volume tiers or negotiated per job?
29. Show a partial payment, deposit and refund. Who confirms money actually arrived?
30. What is the policy for overdue credit, disputed invoices and releasing work before collection?
31. What is the next useful reminder for each client? Who should own it, and what should happen if they do not respond?
32. Which messages are service-related and which are marketing? How are permission, preference and opt-out recorded?
33. Which channel should be integrated first, if any? Who approves each send and its cost?

## Garage / collision branch

34. Who records existing damage and the customer's complaint? What photos/checks are mandatory?
35. Who approves the original estimate and supplementary work? How is the exact revision proven?
36. Which work stages and bays match the real workshop? What typically blocks each stage?
37. Are parts consumed from stock, bought for one job, supplied by the customer or subcontracted?
38. How are warranty rework, paint/material usage and subcontract labour charged and costed?
39. For insurance: who pays whom, what is the customer share, what does the surveyor require and when does settlement usually occur?
40. Which checks and signatures are required before handover? Who may override the payment policy?

## Showroom branch

41. New, used or both? Two-wheelers, cars or commercial vehicles? Owned stock, consignment or both?
42. How are enquiries assigned, followed up and marked lost? Which sources produce sales?
43. What uniquely identifies a vehicle? Can it move between yards before reservation?
44. When does a booking become exclusive? What deposit/refund terms apply?
45. How do finance approval, lender disbursement, customer balance and title transfer differ?
46. Which registration fees, discounts, accessories and tax treatments need separate invoice lines?
47. Do trade-ins, consignment or the used-goods margin scheme apply? These need a dedicated reviewed workflow.
48. What must be complete at handover, and how are returns/re-sale handled after an invoice?

## Retail / trading branch

49. How many items and variants exist? Are SKU, barcode, serial, batch, expiry or unit conversions required?
50. Which customers use wholesale/item-specific prices? Are negative stock and backorders ever allowed?
51. How do staff receive partial deliveries and match purchase orders, supplier bills and payments?
52. What makes a returned item sellable, damaged, warranty stock or a supplier return? Who authorises it?
53. Are transfers between locations required? Which location owns a receipt, sale and returned item?
54. Which catalogue categories and frequently sold items would shorten counter work?

## Service / repair branch

55. Workshop visits, customer-site visits or both? How are time windows and travel charges agreed?
56. Which assets, warranties, access instructions and service history must the technician see?
57. What proves the work was completed and accepted? Which checks differ by service type?
58. Are visits recurring, usage-based, seasonal or under a contract? Who decides the next due date?
59. What happens if parts or customer approval are missing during a visit?
60. Are route planning, customer booking links or service signatures necessary for the first pilot, or simply useful later?

## Staff, commissions and finance

61. How are attendance, paid leave, overtime and salary advances actually calculated and approved?
62. Are commissions fixed, revenue-based, margin-based or collected-payment-based? Show the latest disputed calculation.
63. What happens to a commission after a refund, cancellation, shared sale or employee departure?
64. Which payroll/tax/contribution calculations must remain in an accountant's existing system?
65. What opening balances, stock quantities and records can be trusted? Who will sign off the import?
66. Which reports need exact accounting reconciliation, and which are operational estimates?

## Acceptance and commercial fit

67. Which five tasks must an ordinary staff member complete without training help?
68. What single mistake would make you stop using the app immediately?
69. What outage and data-loss limits are acceptable? Who can perform and verify a restore?
70. What customer data must be retained, exported, corrected or restricted, and for how long?
71. What measurable improvement would justify paying after the pilot? What would make you reject a paid offer?
72. Who will approve the final configuration, sample tax documents, opening data and live-use start date?

## Turn answers into configuration

| Business | Country / registration | Profile / speciality | Daily priorities | Work stages / checks | Client terms / extra fields | Devices / print | Unsupported exceptions | Acceptance owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Fill during observation | Accountant reviewed | Choose one starting profile | Three concrete actions | Use actual vocabulary | Only necessary data | Actual models | Scope explicitly | Named role/person |

For every proposed feature record: the latest example, frequency, time/money lost, existing workaround, who benefits, permission required, exceptional cases and how success will be checked. Build recurring patterns into shared configuration. Quote one-off integration work separately.
